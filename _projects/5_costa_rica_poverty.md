[This project](https://github.com/andrewjtdunn/Costa-Rican-Household-Poverty-Level-Prediction) classifies household-level poverty in Costa Rica from survey data on housing conditions, assets, and household composition, working through the class imbalance and mixed feature types that make the problem harder than it first appears.

It also revisits ground I first covered on foot: as an undergraduate I spent a study abroad semester in Costa Rica interviewing rural rice farmers about the economic effects of CAFTA.
