[Travel Maps](https://github.com/andrewjtdunn/travel-maps) turns a month of raw phone location history into a set of maps tracing a long road trip.

A small exercise in the parts of data work that don't usually show up in a portfolio: getting messy personal data out of an export format that wasn't designed to be read, cleaning coordinates and timestamps, and finding a visual form that makes a month of movement legible at a glance.
