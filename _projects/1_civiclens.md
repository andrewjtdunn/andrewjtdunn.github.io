[CivicLens](https://github.com/uchicago-capp-30320/CivicLens) is a platform designed to abstract procedures and bureaucracy from the federal public commenting process. It promotes effective, accessible public commenting while providing transparency into the process, using plain language to discuss federal rulemaking and educating users on how commenting shapes government.

Built by a team of graduate students in the University of Chicago's CAPP 30320 software engineering course. The project ran to roughly 300 merged pull requests across data collection, a Django application, and deployment tooling, and is released under AGPL-3.0.

As of 2025 the project is no longer under active development and the hosted site is offline, but the codebase and documentation remain public for anyone who wants to continue it.
