[This project](https://github.com/uchicago-mscapp-projects/apwhy) analyzes the relationship between a deprivation index — constructed from socioeconomic data across a variety of sources — and instances of eviction in the city of Chicago. The Python application builds an interactive dashboard summarizing how the different factors relate to one another.

Data is pulled and cleaned from several APIs, including the Chicago Data Portal and Google Maps. The repository ships both a methodology document and a full project paper.

Built with Gregory Ho, Santiago Segovia, and Stephania Tello Zamudio.
